# Excel Sales Analytics

A collection of practical **Excel Data Analytics and Business Intelligence projects** focused on sales performance, financial analysis, revenue analysis, KPI reporting, and business decision-making.

---

## Projects

### 1. AtliQ Hardwares – Market P&L Analysis

- **Project objective:**

  **1.** Analyze market-wise **Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin and Gross Margin %**.

  **2.** Compare financial performance across different global markets.

  **3.** Identify markets with differences in sales and profitability.

- **Report:** [Market P&L Analysis](./report1.pdf)

- **Key metrics analyzed:**
  - Net Sales
  - COGS
  - Gross Margin
  - Gross Margin %

- **Purpose of analysis:**  
  Evaluate market-level financial performance and understand variations in profitability across different markets.

### 2. AtliQ Hardwares – Gross Margin % Analysis
